# Triage note: INC-1102

## Summary
Since about 15:00 UTC on 2026-11-08, the vendor's income-document fraud checks (pay stubs) on `doc-verification` have come back inconclusive far more often than normal. The 30-minute inconclusive rate is 22% against a 3% baseline. Inconclusive applications go to `PENDING_DOC_REVIEW` with no automatic decision, and 41 applications are waiting. Severity is SEV3.

## Likely cause
The most likely cause is the vendor's document model v7.2 rollout at 14:30 UTC. This is moderate confidence and based only on correlation. We made no internal deploys in the last 24 hours. Every inconclusive log line is tagged `vendor_model=v7.2`, and the spike started about 30 minutes after that rollout. The reasons given (`font_metadata_mismatch`, `low_resolution`) suggest the new model is stricter or recalibrated rather than seeing a real wave of fraud. That can't be ruled out from this data alone, though: we have one sample application and no breakdown by model version or document source.
